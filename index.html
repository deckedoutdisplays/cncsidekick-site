On your computer

CNC Offsider also runs in a web browser: cncoffsider.expo.app. Sign in with the same account as your phone and your shop syncs across; Pro bought in the phone app works there too.

Opened an email link on a computer?

Sign-up confirmation and password-reset links finish inside the app. Open the email on the phone or tablet where CNC Offsider is installed and tap the link there.
